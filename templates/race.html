Record finish times and status codes here, together with the IRC and YTC ratings this race will be scored on.

GPS finish detection

Arm GPS auto-finish lets the app take a boat's finish once its tracker has rounded every mark and crossed the line. It is on for a new race: a detection is worth more than a finish you were going to time anyway, and a missed one cannot be recovered afterwards.

Auto-confirm records those finishes without anybody approving each one. It is off for a new race, so each detection waits here for a Confirm or a Dismiss. Turn it on for a race with nobody in the hut — that is what it is for, and the on-the-water page turns it on by itself for a race started from a boat.

The Class column shows the series rating band a boat is scored in, the same one the Add entries tab shows, not whatever class is typed on its boat record.

Postpone (AP)

Postponing a race

If the wind dies before the start, postpone — do not move the start time. Moving it signals nothing to the fleet, and if the sequence is already running it drops the rest of it: boats hear a warning, then no preparatory signal and no gun.

Flying AP sounds two horn blasts, holds the sequence, and shows AP on the race sheet, the competitor pages and the clubhouse display.

Which signal

AP — the warning is made one minute after AP comes down and the gun five minutes after that, so lowering at 14:20 puts the warning at 14:21 and the first gun at 14:26. Whole minutes only, as with the first warning signal.

AP over H — further signals ashore. There is no one-minute rule to count from, so give the time the sequence will restart at instead.

AP over A — no more racing today, so the race is not resumed.

Once the race has started

AP postpones races that have not started. Stopping a race already under way is abandonment — flag N — which this app does not signal. If the wind has died mid-race, shorten the course instead.
