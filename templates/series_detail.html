How the series is scored

Low-point scoring, using this series' discard profile. A race counts if it had entries in the relevant IRC or YTC class, even if nobody in that class finished.

Series ties are broken under RRS Appendix A8; ties within a single race use Appendix A7 equal-points scoring.

The series is flagged as constituted once it has reached the configured minimum number of included races. Overall tables appear where classes under the same rating rule share one start.
