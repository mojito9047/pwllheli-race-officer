Common links

The screens that usually live somewhere other than this one.

  • Split screen The race-office app and the competitor page side by side, for a wide monitor in the hut.
  • Clubhouse display The full-screen chart for the television in the bar. Open it once and leave it — it follows the current race.
  • Competitor page What competitors see: the same page on a phone, a tablet or a PC. No login needed.
  • Set a mark position Taken out in the RIB to re-measure a mark that has dragged. Needs the club's https address rather than a LAN one: a browser will not give a position to an insecure page.
  • Trackers Which tracker is on which boat, and whether each one is reporting.
  • Hut power history Battery, solar and consumption over time.
