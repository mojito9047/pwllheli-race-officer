What the recommendation is built from

The boat polar selected below, the live start-hut wind when it is available, and a matching -SailChart file from data/sailcharts. Where no sail chart matches the polar, the default chart is used instead.

How the courses are ranked

The CHPSC wind band comes first, then the polar-predicted time against the target duration, then the quality of the first leg, then how many sail changes the course asks for.

The estimated time is worked out from mark-to-mark bearings and distances, using VMG rather than straight-line speed on the legs that are very close upwind or deep downwind — where a boat cannot sail the rhumb line and the honest answer is slower than the geometry suggests.
