What a series does

A series groups races together and carries a score across the season, dropping each competitor's worst results according to its discard profile.

The rating-band classes and the default start plan belong to the series, and every race added to it inherits them — which is why a race is joined to a series rather than carrying its own copy of the setup.

Everything here can be changed later under Edit series details. Changing the discard profile or the rating bands re-scores every race in the series at once, so it is a bigger change after a season has started than before one.
