After heavy weather, re-measure

A laid mark drags in a storm, and the app then goes looking for boats rounding a buoy that is no longer there. A mark 50 m adrift is enough to stop it seeing the rounding at all, which holds up every GPS finish behind it.

So after a blow, take a phone out to the ones that have moved and use Set a position from the water.
