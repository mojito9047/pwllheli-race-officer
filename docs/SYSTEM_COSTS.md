Prices as of 6 October 2026.

Equipping the race hut and the yacht trackers cost £5,036 (£4,126 hut, £910 trackers). Running it all costs about £400 a year (£34 a month), plus the Virtual Race Officer's ongoing model cost, which is not yet known.

## Expanding the tracker fleet

Each extra ATC700 costs about £62 plus delivery, and about £11 a year to run, or about £6 if its SIM is paused over winter.

The figures use the August 2026 prices. An ATC700 from White Label GPS is £52 + VAT (£62.40), and delivery is £9.90 per order. The 3 spare Hologram SIMs cover the first 3 trackers; after that, a 10-pack of SIMs cost £57.74 delivered. Running costs are $1 per SIM a month plus about 7 MB of data ($0.21), the fleet's August average per SIM: about £0.91 a month per tracker.

| Trackers added | Trackers + delivery | SIMs | One-off total | Running, all year | Running, Apr–Oct only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | £197.10 | Spares on hand | £197.10 | about £33 | about £19 |
| 5 | £321.90 | New 10-pack, £57.74 | £379.64 | about £55 | about £32 |
| 10 | £633.90 | New 10-pack, £57.74 | £691.64 | about £109 | about £64 |

Traccar, the relay and the hut link should take more boats at no extra cost. The ATC700 charges over USB-C (chargers not costed) and runs about nine hours on a charge, so long and night races need the GL521MGs or an ATC700 on a USB-C power bank (with its power bank setting switched on). Slowing the reporting rate only stretches the battery usefully at every 5 minutes or longer (1.1–1.7% an hour, measured parked), and fixes that far apart are too coarse to detect a finish.

## Reducing running costs

Seasonal changes to the two SIM plans save money without losing anything during the season. The relay server can be cut further when its contract renews in July 2027.

| Cost | What to do | Saving |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hut 4G SIM, £216 a year | Out of season (Nov–Mar), move the SMARTY line to a small-data plan instead of cancelling, so power monitoring and backups keep running. SMARTY is rolling monthly, with no contract. On quiet days the hut uses about 0.25 GB, so the plan needs about 8 GB a month. | Up to £90 a year, less the smaller plan's cost |
| Hut 4G SIM | In season, keep Unlimited. The RUTX11 measured 34 GB in September (27 GB sent, 7.5 GB received): about 0.25 GB on quiet days and 1–2 GB on busy ones. | None: Unlimited fits the use |
| Live stream | Measured on the relay at 1.4 Mbit/s from the camera's 1280×720, 10 fps sub-stream: about 640 MB for each hour someone watches. It ran 82 minutes on 3 Oct (from 11:38) and 24 minutes on 4 Oct, about a quarter of the hut's upload on both days. Setting the sub-stream to 704×576 at 512–768 kbit/s would roughly halve it. | Halves the stream's data; no cash saving while on Unlimited |
| Hut 4G SIM | If the clubhouse broadband can reach the hut over a point-to-point Wi-Fi link, the SIM can go. This depends on line of sight, and the link hardware is a one-off cost. | Up to £216 a year |
| Relay server, £104.40 a year | Before the July 2027 renewal, compare Fasthosts VPS 4 with what the relay needs (about 1 vCPU, 512 MB–1 GB RAM, 4 GB disk), then move to a smaller plan or a cheaper provider. Keep it off a member's home server. | Depends on the plan chosen |
| Tracker SIMs, about £75 a year | Pause the 7 Hologram SIMs over winter: the $1 per SIM monthly fee is about 85% of the cost. Check that paused SIMs are not charged the fee. Keep the 3 spare SIMs unactivated. | About £25 a year |
| Cloudflare | Let Cache Reserve end on 22 Oct as scheduled. Add an R2 lifecycle rule for past seasons' public clips (about 3.5 GB a year) so storage stays inside the free allowance. | Keeps it at about £0 |
| Teltonika RMS | Let the credit lapse on 24 Oct without renewing. | Keeps it at £0 |

The VRO's ongoing cost should be small. The test runs put it at about 1.6 US cents per sentence before caching, on Sonnet 5. That is under $1 on a busy race day, and perhaps $20–50 a season. Prepaid AI Gateway credits cap it, so top up in small amounts.

## Development with Claude

Building the app with Claude Code used about 8.1 billion tokens from 28 July to 6 October 2026. At pay-as-you-go API prices that would be about $5,170 (about £3,900). It was paid for by subscription instead, not per token.

| Model | Replies | Output tokens | Cache reads | Cache writes | API-equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Opus 5 | 14,679 | 10.7 M | 7,341 M | 97.1 M | $4,909 |
| Claude Opus 5.5 | 1,288 | 1.1 M | 522 M | 6.6 M | $178 |
| Claude Fable 5.1 | 245 | 0.4 M | 82 M | 1.2 M | $63 |
| Claude Opus 4.8 | 46 | 0.03 M | 26 M | 0.6 M | $20 |
| **Total** | **16,258** | **12.3 M** | **7,972 M** | **105.4 M** | **$5,170** |

Nearly all the tokens are cache reads: each reply re-reads the session so far, at a tenth of the fresh-input price or less. August was the busiest month, at $3,006 of the total.

The count covers only the session transcripts kept on the development PC, which start on 28 July. Earlier development is not included, so the true token total is higher.
