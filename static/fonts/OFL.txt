Bundled typefaces
=================

The .woff2 files in this folder are redistributed under the SIL Open Font
Licence, Version 1.1, which permits bundling and redistribution with or without
modification, provided the fonts are not sold on their own and this notice
travels with them.

  Newsreader       Production Type            https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Newsreader
  Archivo Narrow   Omnibus-Type               https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Archivo+Narrow
  IBM Plex Mono    IBM / Bold Monday          https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono

Full licence text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

They are served from the app rather than a CDN so that a race sheet renders
identically on the hut PC, on the bar television and on a competitor's phone,
with no external request in the path.
